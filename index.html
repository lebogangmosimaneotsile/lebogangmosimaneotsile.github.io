About

One question, three disciplines.

I believe the best digital products begin with understanding people. My background in Psychology and Sociology taught me how people think, behave and make decisions. Learning front-end development showed me how digital products are built. UX became the place where those two worlds met.

Today I design digital experiences while also writing about the systems that shape technology, behaviour and society — through Observation Lab.

UX Design · Usability Testing · Accessibility · Google UX Certificate Project

Banking Without Borders — Mobile-First Bank Account Setup

Designed a mobile-first onboarding experience for two underserved user groups in South Africa — first-time account holders and foreign nationals. The redesign addressed both logistical barriers (no branch access, document confusion) and psychological ones (fear of rejection as a foreign national). Two rounds of usability testing validated the approach.

"Everything makes sense. Looks very easy to use. Better than ABSA dare I say."— Usability test participant

What I believe

I believe good design starts long before pixels.

It starts with asking better questions.

The best products aren't simply usable.

They're thoughtful. They understand people, respect context and recognise that every interface sits inside a larger social system.

That's the kind of work I want to contribute to.
